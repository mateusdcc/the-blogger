Narcissism is usually described as loving oneself too much. This is tidy, and
therefore suspicious. The narcissistic structure often looks less like an excess
of self-love than a failure of self-rest. The self cannot stay with itself
without importing confirmation from the outside.

Admiration arrives, but it does not nourish. It only postpones collapse.

## The mirror debt

The narcissistic exchange is recursive:

- I perform superiority to obtain admiration.
- The admiration confirms the performance, not the self.
- Because the self remains untouched, more performance is required.
- The cost increases because the performance must keep surpassing its previous
  version.

This is why praise can become frightening. Praise attaches to the image, and the
image must then be maintained. The admired person is not liberated by admiration.
They are hired by it.

## The ordinary version

Most people have some smaller version of this mechanism. We want to be loved for
what we are, while quietly editing what can be seen. We want recognition without
exposure. We want intimacy with favorable lighting.

There is no clean exit from this. The best one can do, perhaps, is notice the
moment when the mirror begins negotiating on behalf of the face.
